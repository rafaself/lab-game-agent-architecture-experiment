# Roguelite Progression Design

Use this skill when creating or reviewing upgrades, rewards, or progression within a roguelite run.

- Keep every upgrade's effect distinct and explain how it changes the player's choices or tactics.
- Make offered choices meaningfully different. Identify synergies, tradeoffs, and possible dominant combinations.
- Place rewards where they support the intended run pacing. Treat reward frequency, healing, and power curves as tuning assumptions when play data is unavailable.
- State whether effects stack, how long they last, and what resets at the end of a run when those rules matter.
- Do not add permanent meta-progression or other progression systems unless the user or specification asks for them.

Present proposed upgrades with their effect, intended benefit, relevant tradeoff or synergy, and any balance assumption. Do not claim the set is balanced without play evidence.
